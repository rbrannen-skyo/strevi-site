Privacy policy

Effective November 2026

This policy explains what information Strevi™ LLC collects through getstrevi.com and how we use it. It covers this website only. Customer data processed in Rain by Strevi is governed by each customer's agreement with us.

Who we are

Strevi LLC builds Rain by Strevi, revenue management software for self-storage. Our mailing address is PO Box 755, Draper, UT 84020.

What we collect

When you request a demo or contact us, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, work email, company, role, approximate number of stores, management software, any message you add, and whether you're interested in our founding customer program. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, prepare for a demo or founding-customer conversation, and keep a record of it. Submitting the form does not sign you up for marketing email.

Who we share it with

We share it only with the service providers that run this website for us: Formspree, which delivers form submissions, and our website hosting provider. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep demo and contact requests for 24 months, then delete them, unless you become a customer or ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you've given us, and we'll act on your request within 30 days. If the law where you live gives you additional rights, we'll honor them.

Contact us

Changes to this policy
